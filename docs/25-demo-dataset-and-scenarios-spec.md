## 1. Executive Summary

This document is the data blueprint for KORA Foundation Light. It specifies every synthetic dataset, scenario, persona, and data pattern required to build and present the full demo with no live company data.

This document does not generate SQL, code, CSV files, or Prisma models. It tells developers and designers precisely what synthetic data is required, what structure it must have, what stories it must support, and what it must never contain.

**Three objectives the demo dataset must satisfy:**

**1. Make KORA understandable.** A buyer who sees Foundation Light must leave knowing: what KORA measures, how the score is produced, why methodology transparency matters, and why privacy is part of the product — not a compliance footnote. The data must make these things visible without narration.

**2. Make KORA commercially persuasive.** The dataset tells a story that resonates with CHROs, CFOs, ESG Directors and Founders who invest in people programs and have never seen the real activation picture. The gap between "we run welfare programs" and "we know what that investment actually produces" must be palpable from the data alone.

**3. Make KORA technically buildable with synthetic data.** Every screen in doc 24 must have a data source. Every flow must have a beginning state. Every warning must be triggered by a specific data pattern. Every empty state must have a scenario where it appears. The dataset must leave no screen underpowered.

**The demo before/after story:**

Meridiana Group S.r.l. — the primary demo company — runs 8 welfare and people programs, spends approximately €280,000 per year, has an active HR and ESG team, and genuinely believes its workforce program is working well.

KORA reveals the real picture:
- 64% of all Impact Units are generated by 12% of workers
- The Operations department (40% of the workforce, located at the Plant site) contributes almost nothing to the data
- CONNECTION and LEGACY pillars are barely represented in verified events
- IMPACT events exist but are mostly self-declared — the verification rate is low
- Workers who do engage do not return the following month — continuity is weak
- The Activation Safeguard triggers WARNING: AR = 0.38, MAR = 0.22

KORA then generates specific next actions.

Scenario 2 shows the same company after acting on those recommendations: broader activation, better continuity, partner validation, a cross-company collective initiative, and improved worker adoption through My KORA.

This is the story the dataset must tell.

## 2. Demo Narrative Goals

The synthetic dataset must support six parallel narratives simultaneously. Each narrative must be convincing independently and coherent as a whole.

| Narrative | Core Message | Key Data Needed |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Company** | "KORA shows the real activation picture — not the program budget." | Participation concentration, pillar imbalance, low continuity, Activation Safeguard WARNING, 10-component breakdown, explainability |
| **B. Worker** | "My KORA gives me personal value. My data is mine. My employer sees none of this." | Worker personas with PIB Light, timeline, opportunities, collective events, Dynamic CV, bookings, privacy screen |
| **C. Partner** | "Partners are verified ecosystem actors, not marketplace vendors." | Partner catalog, service types, opportunities, evidence upload, collective initiative capability, advisor validation status |
| **D. Advisor** | "Advisor validation turns self-declared data into trusted intelligence." | Review queue, evidence inspection, eligibility confidence assignment, completed recommendations |
| **E. KORA Admin** | "KORA transforms heterogeneous files into structured, explainable intelligence." | Upload simulation, AI mapping with confidence scores, UEF review with flagged/rejected records, scoring run, explainability |
| **F. Founder** | "This is a market problem. These are the people who have it. This is their willingness to pay." | Founder validation contacts, objections, pilot signals, revenue indicators |

**What the dataset must NOT do:**
- Show individual worker scores to employer roles
- Imply KORA is a performance management or ranking system
- Suggest scores are scientifically certified or empirically validated
- Make partner relationships look transactional or marketplace-like
- Create a gamification or rewards impression
- Use real personal data of any kind

## 18. Privacy and Suppression Rules in Dataset

### 18.1 Dataset Privacy Rules (Absolute)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| No real personal data | All workers are synthetic constructs with fictional names |
| All workers pseudonymized | worker_pseudonym_id = WRK-MERD-[001–250] — no name in analytics schema |

| Privacy group threshold | Default 10 workers — below-threshold groups suppressed |
| Worker booking/contact data | Never visible to employer at any level |
| Dynamic Impact CV | Never visible to employer at any level — not even in aggregate |
| Worker evidence | Private by default — only visible with worker explicit action |
| Sensitive wellbeing metadata | Minimized to category level — no session detail, no diagnosis, no therapist notes |
| Clinical documents | Absolutely excluded — Foundation Light does not accept or store clinical health records |
| Partner request data | Partner receives only minimum data needed to process request (with worker consent) |
| Advisor records | Advisor sees only assigned review records — no access to other company data |
| Analyst access to individual-level records | Pseudonymized operational records only — must not expose identity-linked worker personal layer content (bookings, partner contacts, Dynamic Impact CV, personal plan, consent details, sensitive wellbeing / health-related metadata). Exceptional identity-linked access belongs only to the KORA Privacy Officer / Privileged Audit workflow per doc 24. |
| LIFE / wellbeing Dynamic CV items | Category-level participation only. No diagnosis, clinical detail, therapy content, health outcome, provider notes, or sensitive session information. Worker may exclude entirely. |

## 23. Do-Not-Generate / Do-Not-Include

The following must never appear in the synthetic dataset:

**Personal data:**
- Real names, real email addresses, real phone numbers, real addresses
- Real company names unless clearly fictionalized (names in this doc are fictional)
- Real national ID numbers, tax codes, SPID/CIE identities
- Actual medical diagnoses, therapy records, or clinical health data
- Any externally sourced personal data

**Financial and payment data:**
- Payment records of any kind
- Wallet balances
- KIP points, KORA Impact Points, KORA Impact Pledge execution records
- Cashback, top-up, reward redemption
- FUO movement
- Real fiscal tax conclusions

**Prohibited categories:**
- Disciplinary records
- Performance appraisal data
- Salary data
- Union/employee relations sensitive records
- Actual HR legal cases

**Production data:**
- Real pilot employee data from any company
- Real partner confidential service or pricing data
- Real advisor client records

**Future scope (not to be included or activated):**
- KORA Link hardware simulation with active data
- Full marketplace booking engine with slot inventory
- Real API integrations
- Production worker accounts
- Active KORA Impact Pledge records
- Sector Friction Index
- Territorial Access Index
- CEF (Contribution Event Format) active computation
- Advanced KORA Contribution mechanics beyond Foundation Light companion indicator

## 24. Final Dataset Decision

The Foundation Light demo dataset must be **narrative-first, privacy-safe, and technically complete**.

The synthetic data must demonstrate — without narration, prompting, or excuse — that:

1. **KORA is not a dashboard.** Every output includes an explanation, a methodology version, a confidence score, and a calibration label. The data itself makes KORA's epistemic honesty visible.

2. **KORA detects real organizational activation patterns.** Meridiana's before-state tells a story that every CHRO, CFO and ESG Director will recognize: programs that run, budgets that spend, and an activation picture that only KORA can reveal.

3. **KORA protects workers.** No employer sees individual data. The system enforces this at the data layer. The demo shows the privacy screens, the suppression states, and the worker's personal layer — which is invisible to the company.

4. **My KORA creates personal value.** The eight worker personas demonstrate that KORA gives workers something genuinely useful: a private personal impact layer, real opportunities, verified contribution, and a Dynamic Impact CV they control.

5. **The partner and advisor ecosystem increases trust.** Partners are not vendors. Advisors are not optional extras. The verification chain from event → partner-validated → advisor-stamped → VR-improved is visible in the data.

6. **KORA Contribution shows verified collective impact.** Five collective initiatives, two cross-company programs, and advisor validation demonstrate that KORA extends beyond the company perimeter — and that this creates a distinct, meaningful indicator.

7. **Synthetic data is enough to build a credible demo before live pilot data.** The 29 synthetic datasets, 4 scoring scenarios, 8 worker personas, 12 partners, 3 advisors, and 25 founder contacts provide everything a development team needs to build a demo that is realistic in structure, logic, narrative power and product credibility. The demo should feel realistic in behavior and intelligence flow, without ever implying that synthetic data is live pilot data. Synthetic data is not live pilot data. Demo outputs are illustrative. No empirical validation claim can be derived from synthetic data. All demo surfaces must visibly label synthetic/demo status where appropriate.

**No SQL DDL, production schema, production database provisioning, Prisma models, migrations, live data pipelines, or production code may be generated from this document until Gate 2 is passed.**

**However, local synthetic CSV/JSON seed files for UI prototyping, demo design, and non-production mock data may be generated before Gate 2, provided they remain local, clearly labeled as synthetic, contain no real personal/company data, and do not imply final database structure.**
